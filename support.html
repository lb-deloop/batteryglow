Common questions

I never see the glow.

Alerts only show while your Mac runs on battery. Plugging in stops them. To check that everything works while plugged in, click the battery icon in the menu bar and use the Preview buttons. You can also set the Low and Critical levels there.

How do I stop the red pulse?

Click the menu bar icon and choose Stop Alert Until Plugged In. With Pro, you can also rest your pointer on the notch until the ring fills. The pulse comes back the next time your battery runs low after charging.

My Mac has no notch.

Notch messages still work. They drop down from the top centre of the screen instead.

I bought Pro, but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings from the menu bar icon, go to the Pro tab and click Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I make it start when I log in?

Open Settings, go to General and turn on Launch on login.

Does Smart Quiet listen to my microphone?

No. It only checks whether another app is using the microphone, which means you're probably on a call. Battery Glow never records or hears any audio. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove Battery Glow?

Click the menu bar icon, choose Quit Battery Glow, then drag Battery Glow from your Applications folder to the Trash.
