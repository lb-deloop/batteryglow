Privacy Policy

Battery Glow for Mac · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Battery Glow collects no data. It doesn't track you, has no analytics and sends nothing about you or your Mac anywhere. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What Battery Glow reads on your Mac

To do its job, the app reads a few things locally. They never leave your Mac and are not stored beyond what's listed below.

  • Battery level and charging state, to know when to show an alert.
  • Pointer position, only to notice when you point at the top centre of the screen (the notch).
  • Whether the microphone is in use, if you turn on Smart Quiet, to stay quiet during calls. Battery Glow never records, hears or accesses any audio, and it doesn't ask for microphone permission.
  • Whether the front app is full screen, if you turn on Smart Quiet, to hold alerts while you watch or present.

What Battery Glow stores

Your settings, such as alert levels, colours and notch options, are saved on your Mac in the app's own storage. Removing the app removes them.

Purchases

Battery Glow Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple ID. Battery Glow receives no personal or payment information, only confirmation from Apple that Pro has been purchased. Apple's privacy policy applies to that purchase.

No tracking, no third parties

Battery Glow contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it shares nothing with third parties. Apart from Apple's App Store for purchases, it makes no network connections.

Children

Battery Glow collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
