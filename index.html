Battery Glow

A soft glow around your screen when your battery runs low, so you notice before your Mac goes to sleep.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Free · Pro is a one-time purchase · macOS 14 or later

At 20%: a soft orange glow

The edges of your screen light up, then fade away after a few seconds. Nothing to click, nothing to dismiss.

At 10%: a pulse you can't ignore

A slow red pulse keeps going until you plug in. On your way to the charger? Stop it from the menu bar.

When you plug in

A quick green glow confirms your Mac is charging.

Battery Glow Pro

Alerts that slide out of the notch, a peek at your charge and time left, Smart Quiet during calls and full-screen apps, an unplug reminder at 80%, and your own colours, including Flow. One purchase, no subscription.

Private by design

Battery Glow collects no data. Everything happens on your Mac.
